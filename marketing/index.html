How Intellicore turns events into memory

Every signal — an incident, a cost anomaly, a security finding — becomes a connected node. Not just logged. Linked.

01 — Memory Event Detection

It understands what happened — not just that something happened

A single alert doesn't tell you much on its own. Intellicore looks at everything around it — what came right before, and whether this exact thing has ever happened before.

  • ✓ If one thing usually causes another — like a Friday deploy causing a Monday outage — Intellicore learns that link.
  • ✓ If the same problem happens 3 or more times, it gets a name and becomes a known pattern.
  • ✓ It only acts on its own when it's truly sure — a one-off blip never triggers an automatic fix.
Example: a Friday deploy pattern
1 Someone deploys on a Friday afternoon.
2 The site starts breaking soon after.
3 Memory: this exact chain has happened 4 times before.
4 It suggests a rule: no deploys after 4pm on Fridays.
02 — Checklists are the past. Context is the future.

We don't hand your team a checklist. We tap them on the shoulder.

A written checklist can only repeat what someone thought of on the day they wrote it. Intellicore doesn't maintain a checklist — it tells your team, in the moment: "this exact thing happened before, and here's exactly what fixed it."

  • ✓ No outdated instructions — every suggestion is based on what's actually happening right now.
  • ✓ It only speaks up when it's relevant, and goes quiet the moment it's not.
